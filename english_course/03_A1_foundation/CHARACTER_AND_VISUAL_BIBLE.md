## Art direction (binding for every illustration brief)

- Style: original modern editorial illustration, organic shapes, clean line work, restrained texture, generous negative space.
- Palette: cream backgrounds; warm orange and terracotta accents; soft brown lines; charcoal for detail; one muted green and one muted blue as secondary support. Orange is an accent, never the only correctness cue.
- `no_text_in_image: true` always — real labels live in the app layer.
- Negative prompt base: "photorealism, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, clutter, glossy 3D, stereotypes, distorted hands, duplicate objects".
- Continuity: character sheets A1-CHAR-ILL001–004 (front view), ILL005–008 (expressions: happy, thinking, sorry, waving). Consistent proportions across all chapters.
- Accessibility: key contrasts pass WCAG-AA against cream; scenes readable at small size; no colour-only meaning; alt text written for every brief.

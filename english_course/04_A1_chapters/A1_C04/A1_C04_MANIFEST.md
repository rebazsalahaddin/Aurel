# A1-C04 Manifest — Checkpoint Review 1: Welcome-Day Mission (review chapter, 3 lessons)

Master prompt §9.4. **No new core language.** Everything below is retrieval, integration, transfer, and assessment of Chapters 1–3 targets. No new ledger rows are created by this chapter.

## Mission

Join the event, check in, meet two people, exchange basic details, and introduce one person to another.

**Story frame:** one new morning at the Community House — a second welcome morning for new neighbors. Alex invites the learner to help (helper badge, ILL005/S05). Two new neighbors arrive across the chapter: **Amara Otieno** (checks in at Nina's desk, C4-L2 integrated listening) and **Rafael Costa** (arrives at the door, C4-L3 mission roleplay). Both are one-off figures registered in the bible BEFORE first use. Checkpoint 1 (L3) is the learner's greeter test — the Arc-1 gate.

## Rulings recorded for lens 3 (and owner-note responses)

1. **No new targets, and recombination ≠ new language.** Pronoun/frame recombinations of taught words (e.g., "I speak … and …", "He's a cook.") are integration of taught material, not new targets; no ledger rows are added. "and" rides inside the taught languages frame (established C3-L1).
2. **Integration over volume** (owner note 1: do not stack more maintenance). The chapter carries 16 retrieval items total (top of the 12–16 range), deliberately multi-target; the 15–25% chapter-quiz rule is **subsumed**, not stacked: Checkpoint 1 is 100% cumulative by design and there is no additional chapter quiz. Every checkpoint item carries a `reviews:` field citing prerequisite IDs.
3. **Clinics.** A clinic = one confusion, 6–10 items, 5–8 minutes. Two clinics are fully authored as chapter content (defaults; the app picks adaptively later):
   - **Clinic 1 (L1): be + person-word agreement** — am/is/are, isn't/aren't, with named referents (absorbs C3-CLIN-A "they is/they are").
   - **Clinic 2 (L2): "I'm from ___" vs "I'm a/an ___"** — origin frame vs job frame plus a/an by sound (absorbs C3-CLIN-C and C3-CLIN-D).
   - C3-CLIN-B (his/her) is retrieved inside Checkpoint 1 grammar items instead of a third clinic.
   - **Owner note 3 resolved:** clinic art draws on the fresh A1-C04-ILL block; no C3 allocation is needed.
4. **Checkpoint ID grammar** follows master prompt §10.1: `A1-CP1-*` for all checkpoint items (V/G/LS/RD/CN/W/SM), independent of the `A1-C04-*` practice blocks.
5. **Speaking mission accessibility:** the Checkpoint 1 speaking mission has a tap-based alternative path (C3-L3 RP001 precedent) — completion counts either way; recording is never required.
6. **Sensitive-content carryover:** origins voluntary; never immigration status or documents; `Good night` never appears as greeting or distractor; no numbers >20 in learner content; no required typing; newcomer names/contacts are fiction.
7. **Glue register (L1):** `morning`, `welcome`, `help`, `door`, `fast`, `counts` are receptive scene glue, illustrated or icon-cued, never tested, never targets — continuation of the C3-L1 glue convention (`welcome` was already glue there; `badge` was glue at C1-L2 S12).

## Lesson map (closed manifests)

### L1 — Welcome Back: Hello, Spell It, Say It Again (retrieval part 1 + clinic 1)

- Story re-entry recap carousel, 3 beats (map wall with SIX dots → check-in desk → name badges) + 3 diagnostic recall taps (RC001–003, unscored).
- **Retrieval part 1 (Ch 1 + Ch 2): RT001–008** — greetings/time-of-day, first/last name, best next turn, heard-word spelling, phone digits, register-card detail, repair choice, one supported recording (skip path + privacy note).
- **Clinic 1: CL1-001–008** — model (learning + challenge takes) → perception → choice ×5 → tile order → contextual transfer; help ladder per item; exit criterion (6/7 interactive correct with no hint above rung 2).
- Pause point at minute 8–10; session close with guide prompts (no bank items).
- Audio **AUD001–015** (15) · ILL **001–005** (5) · screens **S01–S09** (9).

### L2 — The Welcome Morning: Sounds, Forms, and Frames (retrieval part 2 + clinic 2 + integration)

- **Retrieval part 2 (Ch 3): RT009–016** — country audio→map, languages frame, jobs image↔word, "Where is ___ from?", possessive his/her, a/an choice, "What do you do?" next-turn, introduce-a-friend chain.
- **Clinic 2: CL2-001–008** — from-vs-a/an (model → choice → tile rebuild → transfer; exit criterion as clinic 1).
- **Integrated listening A1-C04-D01** (25–40 s: learning take + fresh challenge take): Amara checks in at Nina's desk — greeting, name spelling, phone digits, Alex welcomes, Maya introduces Sam ("This is my friend Sam."). LS001–006 (gist ×2, detail ×2, speaker ×1, transfer ×1).
- **Form/profile reading: RD001–006** — the day's sign-in sheet (3 rows: name/digits/message — C2 genre return, now with newcomers' fictional rows) + Amara's profile card (C3 genre return). Two text types.
- **Tile introduction: WR001–004** — three-sentence self-introduction tiles (name/from/job), introduce-a-friend chain, badge-line order, form-row tile fill. No typing.
- **Conversation rehearsal: CV001–006** — best next turn, intent, order, branch — the greeter conversation, rehearsed before L3's roleplay.
- Audio **AUD016–032** (plan ≈17, incl. D01 two takes and RT/clinic stimuli) · ILL **006–011** (6) · screens **S10–S19** (10).

### L3 — The Mission and Checkpoint 1 (roleplay + checkpoint + results + wrap-up)

- **Roleplay A1-C04-RP001** (6–8 turns, §10.9 contract): Rafael arrives at the door — learner greets, checks him in (name spelling + phone digits), asks origin/job, introduces Rafael to Amara, closes. Branching alternative path for non-voice sessions (C3 precedent).
- **Checkpoint 1 (A1-CP1-*)**: vocabulary 12 · grammar 12 · listening 10 across three recordings (greeting exchange Ch1 · check-in digits/spelling Ch2 · origin/job mini-profile Ch3) · reading 6 · discourse 6 · two tile tasks (W001 self-intro; W002 introduce-a-friend) · one speaking mission (SM01, rubric + tap alternative). Time 20–25 min.
  - **Gate:** 80% overall, ≥70% in vocabulary, grammar, listening, and conversation, plus mission completion. Near-pass (70–79% or one weak component) → clinic + **alternate short form** (A1-CP1-B001–015). Below 70% → personalised review route (per-section loop of the weakest spans). Unlimited retries use parallel content (A1-CP1-P001–012 pool + documented regeneration rule).
- Results screen, next-path routing, chapter wrap-up, gate message.
- Audio **AUD033+** (checkpoint's three recordings CP-AUD001–003 + roleplay/model lines) · ILL **012–016** (5) · screens **S20–S28** (9).

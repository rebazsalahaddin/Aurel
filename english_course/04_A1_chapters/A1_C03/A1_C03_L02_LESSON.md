# S13 — G007 teach, part 1: the whole family of *be*

**Teaching sequence (§10.5 required order): illustrated/audio example → meaning question → more examples → highlighted pattern → concise explanation → choice → tile order → contextual use → conversation transfer → later retrieval.**

**1. Example (audio + art):** [ILL: A1-C03-ILL023 | alt: Nina, Leo, Maya, Alex and Sam stand in a row beside the map wall, each with one empty speech bubble]. A1-C03-AUD045 plays five cast lines; each line highlights on screen:

```
NINA:  I'm from Peru.
LEO:   He's from Australia.          ← pointing at Leo? no — speaker shifts:
```
(final script in AUD045 below — the five model lines are: `He's from Australia.` (about Leo) · `She's from Egypt.` (about Maya) · `They're from Canada.` (about Alex) · `They're from Peru and Egypt.` (about Nina and Maya) · `We're from ten countries.` (the class))

**2. Meaning question (tap):** "Sam says: He's from Australia. — WHO is from Australia?" → photo choice: Leo ✓ / Maya / Sam.

**3. More examples (cast facts, one line each):** `She's a teacher.` (Nina) · `He's a cook.` (Leo) · `They're a designer.` (Alex) · `We're a class.`

**4. Highlighted pattern (app-layer tiles, art stays textless):**

| person word | full | short | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I am | I'm | I'm from Peru. |
| you | you are | you're | You're from Mexico. |
| he | he is | he's | He's from Australia. |
| she | she is | she's | She's from Egypt. |
| they (one person, Alex) | they are | they're | They're from Canada. |
| they (two people) | they are | they're | They're from Peru and Egypt. |
| we | we are | we're | We're from ten countries. |

**5. Concise explanation (guide voice, 2 lines):** "One person you point to: he or she. Alex: they. Two people: they. You + other people: we. After he/she/it say **is**. After they/we/you say **are**."

**6–10.** choice → tile order → contextual use → conversation transfer → later retrieval run through PR-G001–014, D01 turns, and the Ch4 quiz plan (logged in the encounter map).

**Meaning question:** "Maya says: OUR teacher. Who is our?" → the class (you + me + friends) ✓ / Maya alone / Nina alone.
**Pattern tiles:** `my · your (C1!) → his · her · their · our` — "the set is complete now."
**Explanation (2 lines):** "his = a man's/boy's, her = a woman's/girl's, their = Alex's AND two people's, our = we+you. The word after says WHAT: name, phone, email, country, job, teacher."

**Meaning question:** "Why an engineer? — because engineer STARTS WITH…" → a vowel sound ✓ / the letter e only / two languages.

# A1-C02-L02 — Lesson 2: Big Numbers and Contact Details

Chapter 2 · Spell It and Share Your Details · Lesson type V+G (assembly steps 3–9)
Core time ≈ 18–20 min · pause at ≈ 10 min (after numbers input, S15) · prerequisites: A1-C02-L01

**Targets:** 3 pattern systems (PAT003 numbers 6–10; PAT004 11–15; PAT005 16–20) + 9 records — Set C2-C contact words (5: `phone`, `phone number`, `email`, `email address`, `address`), Set C2-D email symbols (2: `at`, `dot`), and the two `What's your …?` expansion chunks (attached to the contact set) + 3 grammar records (G004 `Are you…?`; G005 `It's…`; G006 demonstrated imperatives).
**Screens:** S12–S21 (inventory in `A1_C02_MANIFEST.md`). **Assets:** 37 audio scripts, 10 illustration briefs.
**Learner-facing instruction words:** Stage 1–2 controlled lexicon only (`listen`, `look`, `tap`, `choose`, `match`, `say`, `repeat`, `one`, `two`, `again`, `correct`, `try again`). `put in order` / `first` / `next` / `last` were demonstrated at C1-S24 and remain available for ordering items.
**Retrievals harvested this lesson:** `sorry` (warm-up — open item from L01), alphabet families + 0–5 (warm-up), `What's your name?` (expansion frame → G005), `please` (`Repeat, please!`), `thank you` / `You're welcome` `[CHUNK:survival]` (story), `hello`/`good morning` (story), cast names incl. **Sam Rivera** (G-item badge check), `Can you repeat that, please?` (PR-V035), `again` (G030 context).
**Fictional-data rule (binding):** every phone number and email in this lesson is fictional; the learner never enters real data. Sample numbers use **only digits 0–5** (L1-taught) until the new numbers are taught. Standard samples: Maya's phone `5-5-5, 2-0-1` (spoken "five, five, five … two, zero, one"); Maya's email `maya.haddad@aroa.com` (spoken "maya dot haddad at aroa dot com").

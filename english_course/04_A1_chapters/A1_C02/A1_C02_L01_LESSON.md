- **Listening integrity:** every audio item hides its transcript until after the response (`transcript_release`); one free replay, logged, never penalized. Non-audio route: the letter chart (S04) and the digit strip (S10) give a visual route on every alphabet/number item — reported as `visual_route_used`, listening reported `not measured` only when the audio path is skipped entirely.
- **No typing anywhere; tap-only; no drag; no motor-speed scoring; no colour-only meaning** (correctness never rides on orange alone).
- **Speak moments are optional and ungraded**; ASR only lights echo dots; accent, speed, and personality are never criticised. Non-voice alternatives are real interactions, not dead ends.
- **Count scenes carry exact quantities** (briefs demand exact counts and forbid duplicates); alt text states the count, giving an equivalent channel on counting items.
- **Reduced motion:** letter-card flips → crossfades; count pulsing → sequential dot markers; pans → cuts.
- **No real personal data is ever requested** — the learner spells fictional cast names only; contact details (L2+) are fictional by design.

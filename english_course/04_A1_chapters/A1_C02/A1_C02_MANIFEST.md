# A1-C02 Manifest — Spell It and Share Your Details

Arc 1 · Meet and connect · new-language chapter · 4 lessons · 40–60 min core + adaptive review
Follows A1-C01 (complete). Prerequisites: all Chapter 1 targets (ledger `taught`).

**Mission:** Check in at the Community House desk — spell a name, understand and give a fictional phone number and email.

**Can-dos:** C2-1 recognise/say A–Z (L1) · C2-2 spell a name and request repetition (L1) · C2-3 understand/say numbers 0–20 (L1 starts 0–5, L2 completes) · C2-4 exchange simple contact details (L2–L3) · C2-5 ask for slower or repeated speech (L1).

**Story:** The morning after the welcome event (C1-L4), the learner returns to the Community House to register for community classes. **Nina runs the check-in desk.** A name has to be spelled, a (fictional) phone number and email given — and when speech is too fast or unclear, the learner learns to ask again, slowly.

## Targets

- **Pattern system PAT001 (L1):** alphabet `A–Z` — uppercase/lowercase letter names, ONE ledger row; taught in four families (A–F, G–M, N–T, U–Z); GA letter names (`Z` = /ziː/; BrE `zed` tagged variant only); confusable families /iː/ (B C D E G P T V Z) and /ɛ+m,n/ (M N) practised by perception.
- **Pattern system PAT002 (L1):** numbers `0–5` — `zero, one, two, three, four, five`; countable-object scenes; finger counting (Nina's habit); digit↔word both ways.
- **Pattern systems PAT003–005 (L2):** numbers `6–10`, `11–15`, `16–20` — nothing above 20 productively; receptive awareness only that some number words end in a long /iː/ sound (naming NO `-ty` words; the real `-teen/-ty` contrast is C5, rehearsed C8 — §9.2 v2 fix).
- **Micro-set C2-A (6, L1) — spelling and repair words:** `spell`, `repeat`, `slow`, `again`, `listen`, `say` (`listen`/`say` move from demonstrated instruction words to learner content).
- **Micro-set C2-B (3, L1) — repair chunks (chunk set):** `How do you spell that?`, `Can you repeat that, please?`, `Please speak slowly`. Chunk set attached to the repair function — follows the grammar-attached-set ruling (QA report C1, lens 3), not the 5–8 lexical floor.
- **Micro-set C2-C (5, L2) — contact words:** `phone`, `phone number`, `email`, `email address`, `address`.
- **Micro-set C2-D (2+, L2) — email symbols:** `at`, `dot` (hyphen only if a sample needs it).
- **Chunks (L2):** `What's your phone number?`, `What's your email address?`, `It's …` (details sense).
- **Grammar (L2):** G004 `Are you …?` + `Yes, I am / No, I'm not` (concrete confirmation); G005 `It's …` for letters/numbers/contact details; G006 demonstrated imperatives `listen/say/repeat/choose/match` (formal record; demonstrated since C1-S03). Review of C1 throughout.
- **Pronunciation/listening:** alphabet confusables (GA model); letter strings of 3–6; chunked number strings of 3–7 digits; working-memory load kept low (strings ≤6 letters, ≤7 digits, always chunked).
- **Conversation (L3):** 8–10 turns at check-in — greeting/name, spelling request, one repair, phone OR email, thanks, farewell. **Both versions authored** (phone version + email version).
- **Reading/writing (L3):** illustrated form fields; audio-to-letter/number matching; assemble a fictional email (`maya@…` style — fictional only) and `My phone number is …`; **never require real personal data**.
- **Do not teach:** numbers >20; `-ty` words; `Good night`; anything outside the cumulative ledger.

# S14 — Grammar teach 1: whose? — NAME + 's (G010)

**Screen:** the noticing sequence over the album ([ILL: A1-C05-ILL023 | a photo card of Maya linked by a soft arrow to the album on the table]). Three examples play (AUD043), each highlighted: **Maya's album.** · **Leo's sister.** · **Pepper is Leo's dog.**

## Grammar record G010

```yaml
id: A1-C05-L02-G010
title_for_learner: "Whose? — Leo's sister"
creator_label: "singular possessive 's (NAME + 's + thing)"
communicative_purpose: say WHO a person or thing belongs to, in one short phrase
new_dimension: possession shown ON the name, instead of my/your/her (the possessive adjectives of C1/C3)
prerequisite_ids: [A1-C01-L02-G002, A1-C03-L02-G008, A1-C05-L01-V004, A1-C05-L01-V018]
allowed_forms:
  - "NAME + 's + noun: Leo's sister · Maya's album · Noor's photo"
  - "NAME + 's + noun + be: Pepper is Leo's dog."
  - "Is this NAME's noun? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't."   # Is this…? + short answers from G004/G007
not_yet_allowed_forms:
  - "plural possessive (parents' car)"
  - "of-construction (the sister of Leo)"
  - "'s on pronouns (her's)"        # possessive adjectives already cover this
  - "possessive questions with Whose…?"   # 'Whose' is not a taught A1 target in this course
example_first_sequence:
  - "MAYA: This is Leo's sister. Eva."       # hook-adjacent, bible-true
  - "ALEX: Is this Maya's album? … Yes, it is!"
  - "LEO: And this is Leo's dog! … I say: my dog. You say: Leo's dog."
pattern_display: "NAME + 's + THING → the THING of that NAME (in one phrase: Maya's album)"
meaning_explanation: "'s after a name says: this thing or this person BELONGS to the name. Leo's sister = the sister of Leo. Maya's album = the album of Maya."
use_explanation: "Use it when you talk ABOUT a person: their thing, their person, their photo. With MY sister you talk about YOU; with Leo's sister you talk about LEO."
spoken_contractions: ["the 's sounds like /z/ after Leo and Maya: Leo/z/ sister — small and quick, never a full syllable"]
common_errors:
  - "dropping 's: ✗ Leo sister → ✓ Leo's sister (no 's = two names side by side)"
  - "using it like a plural: ✗ two dog's → ✓ two dogs (plural -s is G012, not 's)"
  - "'has 24 years' for age → age uses BE: Eva is 24 years old (pre-empted here, taught in G011)"
practice_ids: [A1-C05-L02-PR-G001…PR-G008]
conversation_turn_ids: [A1-C05-D01-T001, A1-C05-D01-T005, A1-C05-D01-T007]
later_retrieval_ids: [Ch6 "Is this Maya's key?", Ch8 review, L3 quiz 's items]
source_notes: [expert_judgment]
review_status: reviewed
```

## Grammar record G011

```yaml
id: A1-C05-L02-G011
title_for_learner: "have / has — I have a dog"
creator_label: "have/has + don't/doesn't have (possession and relationships)"
communicative_purpose: say what someone has — a dog, an album, a sister, a phone
new_dimension: the verb have with its two present shapes (have/has) and negatives
prerequisite_ids: [A1-C05-L01-V018, A1-C05-L02-G010, A1-C03-L01-V026]
allowed_forms:
  - "I/you/we/they + have: I have a dog. · We have photos… (people photos!) · They have an album."
  - "he/she/NAME + has: Leo has a dog. · Maya has an album. · Noor has six grandchildren? — NO: ages and counts of people stay with be; has takes THINGS (see common_errors)"
  - "negatives: I don't have a dog. · Maya doesn't have a dog. · Leo doesn't have a brother… — see guardrail: only bible-true negatives"
not_yet_allowed_forms:
  - "have got / has got"
  - "Do you have…? / Does she have…? (do-support — returns in C7)"
  - "have for age: ✗ Eva has 24 → ✓ Eva is 24 years old"
  - "have to (obligation), there + have"
example_first_sequence:
  - "LEO: I have a dog! Pepper."                       # bible-true
  - "MAYA: I don't have a dog. I have an album!"       # bible-true (D01)
  - "ALEX: Maya has an album. Leo has a dog."          # report line — Alex comments, is never the haver
pattern_display: "I / you / we / they + HAVE + thing … he / she / NAME + HAS + thing"
meaning_explanation: "have puts a thing in your hands or your life: a dog, an album, a phone. The NAME words (he, she, Leo, Maya) take the s-shape: has."
use_explanation: "Say what people have. For NO, put don't or doesn't before have: I don't have a dog. Maya doesn't have a dog."
spoken_contractions: ["don't = do not · doesn't = does not (said smooth, never spelled out at A1)", "has sounds like /hæz/ — the s is a z-sound"]
common_errors:
  - "✗ She have / ✗ I has → the s-shape goes with NAME words only"
  - "✗ Eva has 24 years → age uses be: Eva is 24 years old"
  - "✗ I not have → the no-word is don't: I don't have"
practice_ids: [A1-C05-L02-PR-G009…PR-G020]
conversation_turn_ids: [A1-C05-D01-T005, A1-C05-D01-T006, A1-C05-D01-T011]
later_retrieval_ids: [Ch6 ownership in the home search, Ch8 have/has clinic option + review, L3 quiz, C9 Do we have…? service]
source_notes: [expert_judgment]
review_status: reviewed
guardrail: >-
  Cast-facts only: Leo has a dog (Pepper) · Maya has an album and a phone (5-5-5 2-0-1) ·
  Nina has a phone and an email · Sam has a phone (4-0-1 7-3-2) · Eva is Leo's sister

  a have/has subject (they/them discipline) · no invented possessions.
```

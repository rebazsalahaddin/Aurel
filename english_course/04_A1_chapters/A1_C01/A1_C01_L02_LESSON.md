**Notice sequence (S13, before any rule text):** play AUD038 → learner taps the *different* word in each pair (am→'m, are→'re); then AUD039 (my/your) with the two badges lighting up; then AUD040 — the full meeting chunk dialogue — with a "who asks?" moment. Only then the pattern display appears (1–3 short lines, Stage 1/2 words only).

**UI/UX tip — S13:** Notice-before-rule: the screen plays first; pattern text reveals only after the learner completes two tap-to-spot interactions. Pattern display = one line per form pair, generous type, contractions joined with "→" (arrow icon also used in S03 demo 4). The chunk dialogue (AUD040) renders as a two-speaker chat with the asking turn outlined — never color-only.

**Guided grammar (S14):** PR-G001–G030 run here and across S18. New Stage-2 words (`correct`, `try again`, `one`, `two`) are demonstrated by the S03-icon engine with one worked example before first use. Immediate neutral feedback; error classification (missing-be, wrong-person, wrong-direction, chunk-swap) routes the specific hint.

**UI/UX tip — S14:** Errors never end an item: wrong tap → the relevant pattern line glows softly + one retry; second miss → hint rung 2 removes one option; third → answer with explanation, then a near-transfer twin item is queued (help ladder §7.11). Keep the pattern display docked (collapsible) during practice so support stays one tap away.

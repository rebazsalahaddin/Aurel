# Mobbin MCP coverage log

Research date: 2026-08-24  
Connection: authenticated Mobbin MCP  
Scope: Aurel's five audited flows, 20 router-level screens, 34 material states, six screen families, and 29 authored presentation kinds

## Method and limits

- **Observed:** The Mobbin MCP connection was live and authenticated. `search_flows` and `search_screens` both returned metadata and inline images during this task.
- **Observed:** 37 searches were executed: 9 flow searches and 28 deep screen searches. They returned 10 flows with 150 total indexed steps and 109 individual screen results. The MCP rendered 40 evenly spaced flow previews and all 109 screen-result images, for 149 inspected image previews in total. Duplicates and irrelevant matches were not silently promoted to evidence.
- **Observed:** Every returned image used below was visually inspected. Observations are based on visible hierarchy, copy, controls, and state—not result titles alone.
- **Observed:** No Mobbin image was copied into the repository. Links below point to canonical Mobbin screen or flow pages. The MCP's short-lived image URLs are omitted because they expire.
- **Limitation:** Mobbin returned no capture date, app version, device settings, interaction recording, accessibility tree, or outcome data. These references establish that a visual pattern has shipped in a cataloged product; they do not establish that it is current, accessible, effective, popular, or appropriate for Aurel.
- **Limitation:** Natural-language retrieval can return adjacent or irrelevant products. Those misses are recorded in the query log and do not support a claim.
- **Limitation:** `search_sections` was not used for the benchmark because it searches website sections, while the declared product scope is a native iPhone app. Its connection was separately verified before this pass.

## Synthesis constraints

Mobbin evidence is used to compare patterns, not to copy branded layouts, wording, illustrations, mascots, scoring systems, or purchase tactics. Apple guidance and Aurel's verified runtime needs remain higher in the source-of-truth hierarchy. Where a Mobbin reference conflicts with Aurel's adult, calm, non-punitive direction—or where Mobbin cannot establish accessibility, platform correctness, transaction behavior, privacy, or learning efficacy—the reference is treated as inspiration or a counterexample, not a requirement.

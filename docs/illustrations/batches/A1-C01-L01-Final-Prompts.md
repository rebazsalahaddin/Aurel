## Shared visual base

Create an original premium contemporary editorial 2D illustration for Aurel, an adult beginner English-learning app. Use believable adult anatomy and proportions; softly geometric faces with modest eyes; restrained, natural expressions; controlled warm-charcoal linework; matte cream, terracotta, sage, mustard, and muted-slate color blocks; a very light paper texture; broad soft shadows; quiet natural light; and background detail clearly subordinate to the educational action. Keep the composition calm, warm, culturally neutral, and immediately readable at small mobile size. Use an exact landscape 16:9 composition. Keep every face, complete hand, target gesture, and target object inside an 8% horizontal and 10% vertical safe zone, with no critical information in the outer corners. No text appears inside the artwork.

Recurring cast continuity is binding:

- Alex: 26, they/them, light-brown skin, short black hair, round glasses, mustard sweater, blue pants, brown backpack.
- Maya: 28, she/her, brown skin, tied-back dark wavy hair, olive jacket, cream top, small star pin.
- Leo: 31, tall, light skin, curly auburn hair and short beard, blue apron over a cream-and-slate striped shirt.
- Nina: 29, light-olive skin, dark hair with a clear grey streak in a low bun, teal cardigan, cream top, dark wide-leg trousers, small closed notebook when specified.

## Universal exclusions

Avoid photorealism, photo-adjacent rendering, publisher or artist imitation, anime, chibi anatomy, oversized eyes, stock-vector corporate styling, waxy or airbrushed skin, glossy 3D, dramatic rim light, clutter, excessive micro-detail, malformed or duplicate anatomy, incorrect finger count, merged hands and objects, duplicate props, impossible perspective, internal frames, borders, captions, letters, numbers, signs, logos, and watermarks. The teaching meaning must be understandable from action, pose, expression, object logic, and environment—not from color alone.

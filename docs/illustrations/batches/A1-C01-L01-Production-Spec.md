## Shared production contract

- **Course objective:** identify and use greetings, farewells, and basic polite words from clear visual context.
- **Target UI:** Chapter 1 card, practice, and review placements rendered by `IllustrationPlaceholder`; exact `16:9`, `scaledToFit`, rounded 20–24 point masks, no cropping.
- **Delivery:** opaque non-interlaced sRGB PNG; 960×540 in the 2x slot and 1440×810 in the 3x slot; no 1x bitmap.
- **Safe zone:** faces, hands, target gestures, and target objects inside 8% horizontal / 10% vertical; critical details outside the outer 12% corners.
- **Approved visual language:** original premium contemporary editorial 2D; believable adult proportions; softly geometric faces; modest eyes; restrained natural expressions; controlled warm-charcoal outlines; matte cream, terracotta, sage, and muted slate color blocks; very light paper texture; broad soft shadows; background detail below subject detail.
- **Continuity:** Alex and Maya must match the approved pilot. Leo and Nina must match the binding character bible and the batch continuity reference. Clothing and canonical accessories remain stable unless a scene specification says otherwise.
- **Universal avoid list:** photorealism, publisher imitation, anime styling, oversized eyes, stock-vector corporate styling, chibi proportions, waxy or airbrushed skin, glossy 3D, cinematic rim light, random micro-detail, clutter, malformed or duplicate anatomy, incorrect finger count, merged hands/objects, duplicate props, impossible perspective, internal frames, decorative borders, text, letters, numbers, signs, logos, and watermarks.
- **Educational test:** the target meaning must be apparent before reading the card word; color may support but never carry the meaning alone.

## Production continuity reference

Create one production-only cast key, not an app asset: Alex, Maya, Leo, and Nina in the approved style, neutral front/three-quarter standing poses, stable height relationships, uncluttered cream ground, no labels or text. Use the approved pilot as the visual reference for Alex, Maya, linework, palette, texture, and facial construction. This key is for later generation consistency and is archived with production records.

## Set B — polite words and clear responses

### A1-C01-ILL008 — “please”

- **Meaning:** a polite request.
- **Scene:** Maya offers one ceramic cup toward Alex with both hands and a warm, gently asking expression; Alex listens attentively with open posture.
- **Target:** Maya’s two anatomically correct hands around the outside of one cup.

### A1-C01-ILL009 — “thank you / thanks”

- **Meaning:** gratitude after receiving something.
- **Scene:** Alex receives one cup from Maya and places the free hand lightly at their chest in gratitude; Maya has just released the cup.
- **Target:** clear transfer, only one cup, no overlapping or merged fingers.

### A1-C01-ILL010 — “sorry”

- **Meaning:** apology for a small accident.
- **Scene:** Leo looks concerned and raises one open apologetic hand while his closed notebook has just fallen beside his foot; Nina is present but secondary and unhurt.
- **Target:** apology expression and gesture, notebook clearly separate from feet.

### A1-C01-ILL011 — “excuse me”

- **Meaning:** politely asking to pass.
- **Scene:** Nina angles sideways through a narrow park path between two adults, one open hand indicating her intended path; the other people make space.
- **Target:** movement corridor and respectful gesture; no collision or distress.

### A1-C01-ILL035 — “yes”

- **Meaning:** clear positive response.
- **Scene:** one unnamed adult in a waist-up portrait nods once and gives one unmistakable thumbs-up with a relaxed affirmative expression.
- **Target:** single hand, correct thumb orientation; meaning must not rely on green.

### A1-C01-ILL036 — “no”

- **Meaning:** clear calm negative response.
- **Scene:** the same unnamed adult in matching waist-up framing gently turns their head side-to-side and raises one open refusal hand.
- **Target:** five-finger palm and calm boundary-setting expression; meaning must not rely on red.

## Batch QA gates

For each image: inspect faces, hairline, ears, eyes, mouth, finger count, grip/contact, shoulder/limb connections, object count and geometry, floor/path/door perspective, text artifacts, edge safe zones, exact ratio, color profile, and small-screen legibility. Reject or correct failures before integration. After Set A and Set B, compare the full contact sheet for character, line, palette, shadow, texture, perspective, and detail-density drift. Build the app and capture representative card/practice/review placements before marking the batch complete.

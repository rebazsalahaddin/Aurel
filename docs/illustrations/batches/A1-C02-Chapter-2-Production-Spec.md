# Aurel Illustration Production Specification — A1-C02 “Spell It and Share Your Details”

**Status:** Frozen for production — 2026-08-29  
**Scope:** 35 live assets, `A1-C02-ILL001` through `A1-C02-ILL035`  
**Approval:** User explicitly approved Chapter 2 production on 2026-08-29  
**Reference standard:** the approved A1-C01 editorial system and canonical cast key

## Shared production contract

- **Learning mission:** check in at the Community House desk; spell a name; understand and give fictional phone numbers and email addresses.
- **Visual language:** original premium contemporary editorial 2D; believable adult proportions; warm-charcoal linework; matte cream, terracotta, sage, and muted-slate color blocks; restrained paper texture; quiet daylight; graphically clear at phone size.
- **Frame:** exact 16:9 landscape. Faces, hands, target objects, and teaching surfaces stay inside 8% horizontal / 10% vertical safe zones and outside the outer 12% rounded-mask corners.
- **Delivery:** opaque, non-interlaced, sRGB PNG; 960×540 in the 2x slot and 1440×810 in the 3x slot; no 1x bitmap.
- **Text policy:** no embedded text, letters, numbers, names, labels, phone numbers, email addresses, logos, or watermarks. Alphabet, number, badge, register, email, and form content belongs to native app-layer text.
- **Exact-count policy:** count illustrations must contain exactly the authored count, with clearly separated objects and no decorative duplicates that could be mistaken for the target.
- **Accessibility:** each image must remain semantically diagnostic without color; live course alt text is the accessible equivalent.
- **Negative base:** photorealism, anime, chibi proportions, oversized eyes, glossy 3D, cinematic glow, saturated rainbow color, stereotypes, clutter, malformed hands, duplicate limbs, duplicate or fused objects, illegible pseudo-text.

## Rejection gates

Reject and regenerate an asset if any of the following occurs: incorrect object count; character identity drift; wrong clothing or skin tone; malformed or ambiguous hands; visible pseudo-letters/digits; names or contact details baked into pixels; cropped target; an answer cue dependent on color; inconsistent Community House architecture; internal frame/border; watermark; non-16:9 composition.

## Production ranges (binding)

- 220–320 active lexical items/chunks (pattern systems — alphabet, number ranges, clock times, prices — get one ledger row each, not cards).
- ~24 grammar micro-targets in the §8.2 order.
- 9 new-language dialogues + 3 cumulative missions; turn counts per the §7.7 table.
- Per new-language chapter: 4–6 comprehension-listening assets + one word-model asset per vocabulary record + one blended review asset per micro-set.
- ≥9 reading genres, ≥9 guided writing tasks; 3 checkpoint roleplays + 1 final integrated sequence.
- Practice-bank minimums per §10.2 distributed across each chapter's lessons.

## Standing setting

Fictional walkable city **Aroa**: home, café, park, shop, station, and the Community House (welcome-event hub for Arc 1). All names, phones, emails, addresses, maps, and prices are fictional. Café serves non-alcoholic items only.

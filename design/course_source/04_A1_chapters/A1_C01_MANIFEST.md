# A1-C01 Manifest — Hello! My Name Is Alex

Arc 1 · Meet and connect · new-language chapter · 4 lessons · 40–60 min core + adaptive review
Public-quality benchmark / free trial chapter (master prompt §7.13).

**Mission:** At the Community House welcome event, greet a recurring character, exchange names, ask how the person is, and close politely.

**Can-dos:** C1-1 time-appropriate greetings/farewells (L1) · C1-4 basic politeness (L1) · C1-2 say/ask a name (L2) · C1-3 ask/answer `How are you?` (L2).

**Prerequisites:** none (first chapter).

**Story:** Alex Kim volunteers at the Aroa Community House welcome event; Maya arrives early to help. Learner "arrives" at the event and learns to greet, thank, apologise, and say goodbye.

## Targets

- **Micro-set A (8, L1):** `hello`, `hi`, `good morning`, `good afternoon`, `good evening`, `goodbye`, `bye`, `see you`
- **Micro-set B (7, L1):** `please`, `thank you`, `thanks`, `sorry`, `excuse me`, `yes`, `no`
- **Micro-set C (5, L2):** `good`, `fine`, `okay`, `great`, `not bad`
- **Micro-set D (7, L2):** `name`, `first name`, `last name`, `My name is …`, `I'm …`, `What's your name?`, `Nice to meet you`
- **Micro-set E (3, L2):** `How are you?`, `I'm good/fine/okay`, `And you?`
- **Grammar (L2):** G001 `I/you` + be affirmative + contractions; G002 `my/your` + `name`; G003 name/state chunks (chunks only, no analysis); capital `I`, names, period/question mark (recognition)
- **Pronunciation (L2):** statement vs friendly-question intonation; contractions `I'm/you're/What's`
- **Listening:** identify greeting, name, state, farewell across 2–4 turns; learning + challenge takes
- **Conversation (L3):** 6–8 turns — approach, greet, exchange names, ask state, close; two politeness expressions; safe fictional names
- **Reading (L3):** name badges (2), two-line welcome card, greeting-by-time matching
- **Guided writing (L3/L4):** tiles for `My name is Alex.` and `Nice to meet you.` (+ 4 more tile/punctuation items in L4 practice)
- **Do not teach:** `Good night` as a greeting; anything outside the cumulative ledger

## Quiz/gate

22-item mixed quiz (±2): vocabulary 6, grammar 6, listening 4, reading 2, discourse 2, guided writing 2 — tap-first, cumulative share 4–5 items. Low-stakes spoken mission separate. Gate: 80% overall, ≥70% per core section; near-pass → clinic + alternate items; below → personalised review; unlimited parallel retries; no permanent lock.

## Accessibility alternatives

Every listening item: transcript released only after scored response; replay allowed once by default (logged, unpenalized). Non-audio route reports listening `not measured`. All practice items tappable; no drag precision; no colour-only meaning; Dynamic Type + VoiceOver order tested per screen tip; reduced motion and mute respected.

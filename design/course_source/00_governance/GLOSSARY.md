# GLOSSARY.md — load-bearing terms (master prompt §10.0)

- **item** — one scored or practised task: prompt, options, key, rationale. Not an audio asset.
- **asset** — one distinct audio recording (scripted here) or one illustration brief. Ratio rule: one listening asset may serve 2–4 items. Comprehension-listening budget: 4–6 assets per new-language chapter. Word-model assets (single-word audio on vocabulary cards) are budgeted separately: one per vocabulary record plus one blended review asset per micro-set.
- **encounter** — one meaningful, planned contact with a target (input, practice, conversation, assessment, review).
- **screen** — one learner-facing app view; enumerated per chapter in its manifest (§6.4 UI/UX tips).
- **micro-set** — 5–8 new items taught together; v1 groups of <5 merge, groups >8 split (§7.3).
- **pattern system** — a generative rule taught once with examples (alphabet, number ranges, clock times); one ledger row each.
- **survival chunk** — fixed phrase used before its grammar is taught; marked `[CHUNK:survival]`.
- **clinic** — focused 5–8-minute remediation sequence of 6–10 items on one confusion.
- **testlet** — 2–4 items sharing one stimulus with documented dependence.
- **form** — one complete assembly of an assessment. Form A: in-chapter. Form B: parallel form, generated in a dedicated follow-up session on owner request (`form B`).
- **delivery_style** (audio) — `learning_slow_clear` (instruction/model takes) | `challenge_natural_slow` (fresh equivalent takes, slightly faster, reduced support) | `level_natural` (later levels).
- **gate** — chapter completion threshold: 80% overall and no core section below 70%; near-pass 70–79% routes to clinic + alternate form; below 70% routes to personalised review; unlimited parallel retries; never a permanent lock.
- **sanctioned placeholder** — exactly two forms: `[ILL: …]` tokens and `status: placeholder` in placement stubs. Everything else must be fully authored.
